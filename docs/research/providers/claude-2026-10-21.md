## Contract and limits

The adapter accepts a bounded JSON envelope with `local_command: usage`, `num_turns: 0`, `duration_api_ms: 0`, `total_cost_usd: 0`, `is_error: false`, and a text `result`. It reads only lines of the form `Current session: N% used`, `Current week (all models): N% used`, and optional model-specific `Current week (...)`. A new monthly row requires separate qualification. Active rows require `· resets <local date/time> (<time zone>)`; 0%-used rows may omit it. Unknown `Current ...` rows, duplicate rows, malformed percentages, unknown time zones, or missing short/weekly rows make the entire poll unavailable. The percent is the client's displayed integer; remaining is `100 - N`. Exact hidden rounding is not asserted.

No direct HTTP call, credential file read, transcript read, or undocumented cache is part of this contract. Anthropic documents the [CLI print/JSON/no-persistence flags](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/cli-reference) and [status-line rate-limit fields](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/statusline); the exact non-interactive `/usage` text is version-pinned observed client behavior, not a published stable schema. Independent implementations such as [UsageDeck](https://github.com/CallMeLewis/UsageDeck) and [Clawdometer](https://github.com/cthpAiden/Clawdometer) also use this official-client output.
